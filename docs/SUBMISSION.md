# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Submission checklist

**Team/project:** EarthBeat — Hear the Earth. Understand its changes.

**Challenge:** The Earth Information Jukebox

## Project summary (English)

EarthBeat is an interactive, bilingual sound experience that transforms twelve annual NASA Scientific Visualization Studio nighttime-lights frames (2012–2023) into dynamically synthesized music. Visitors can listen to annual changes, examine the image-derived visual indices, and compare years using the same deterministic mappings. The experience aims to make NASA's Earth visualization more accessible and engaging without presenting the resulting musical notes as measured physical sound or calibrated satellite radiance.

## Suggested 30-second demonstration

- **0–6 sec:** Open the EarthBeat map. "What if you could hear NASA's Earth data?"
- **6–16 sec:** Hit Play, show the scanner and explain that the notes follow actual pixels of the NASA visualizations.
- **16–24 sec:** Compare 2012 with 2023 and show the visual indicators.
- **24–30 sec:** Highlight accessibility, scientific attribution, project name and the live URL.
